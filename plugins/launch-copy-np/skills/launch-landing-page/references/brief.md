# Brief and message brief

## Brief fields

| Field | Role in drafting | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| Product in one sentence | Minimum: known or clearly inferable | "Sathi Khata keeps a shop's udharo, stock and daily sales on an Android phone." |
| Audience | Helpful context; needed when the requested copy depends on audience fit | "Kirana and hardware shop owners in Rupandehi who give goods on credit to regular customers." |
| Intended actions | Needed for requested action copy; allow plan or audience routes | "Start the 30-day free trial." |
| The reader's current alternative | Helpful context; omit unsupported comparisons | "A paper khata notebook; reminders by phone call or in person." |
| Supplied facts | Enough relevant facts to support the requested copy; no quota | "Works offline and syncs later"; "Sends SMS reminders to customers the shopkeeper chooses"; "Nepali and English interface"; "212 shops in the pilot" |
| Proof assets | Optional unless the requested section needs evidence; omit unsupported proof | "Two shop owners' quotes with permission, name and town", or "none" |
| Constraints | optional | Length, words to avoid, legal lines the user must include |
| Voice profile | optional | From brand-voice |

Draft when the known product, page purpose and supplied facts support the requested copy without implying unknown material terms. Never turn unknown product facts into assumptions. Omit unsupported statements; mark or briefly note only missing details material to use, fit, price or cancellation. Ask first only when the product or page type is unknown: one short list, most important first.

## Message plan (for yourself; show it only when the user asks for a message brief)

1. **Positioning line:** For [audience] who [current alternative and its cost], [product] [what it does], unlike [alternative], [differentiator from the facts].
2. **Hierarchy:**
   - Main message: one sentence.
   - Distinct supporting points as warranted by the facts and reader decisions, each with its supporting fact or evidence. Do not force a minimum or maximum.
3. **Action hierarchy,** with clearly named next steps appropriate to campaign, plan comparison or audience routing.
4. **Provisional nonfactual drafting choices and unresolved material facts.** Keep unsupported product and audience claims out of public copy.

## Rules

- Every supporting point maps to a brief fact. A point with no fact is dropped or listed under Gaps, never filled with an invented detail.
- No invented numbers, quotes, logos, customer names, deadlines, stock levels or discounts.
- If the brief itself contains a claim with no basis (for example "the only app a shop will ever need"), keep it out of the draft and list it for copy-proof-check.
