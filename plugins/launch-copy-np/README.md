# Nepal Launch Copywriting

Part of [Product Launching](../../README.md), Claude skills for launching products in Nepal, by ICT Matrics.

## What it does

Launch copywriting for the Nepal market, built on the fact-locked Copywriting Editor Kit: draft landing, product, pricing and about pages from your facts; edit copy with every price, date and claim kept; diagnose why a page does not convert for Nepali buyers (price, delivery, COD, returns, address, trust); check claims like 'No. 1 in Nepal', '100% original' and 'organic' against your evidence with Nepal rule flags; capture a brand voice covering Nepali, English and Romanized language mix; and adapt copy between English, Nepali and code-mixed Nepali without translation-speak.

## Skills

Each skill is chosen by what you give it:

| You give | Skill |
|---|---|
| A brief or product facts, no page yet | `launch-landing-page`: Page draft in English, Nepali or code-mix with real price, payment, delivery and return terms; gaps listed |
| Your draft + 'edit' or 'tighten' | `copy-edit`: Edited text first, facts locked; change notes on request |
| An existing page (URL or pasted) | `copy-diagnose`: What to fix first for Nepali buyers, top changes, optional score |
| Copy + your evidence | `copy-proof-check`: Claims ledger with status, evidence line, honest rewrite and Nepal rule flags |
| Your writing samples, or an interview | `brand-voice`: Voice profile with language mix, address (tapai/hajur), festival style, words to use and avoid |
| Copy + target language | `bilingual-copy`: English ↔ Nepali ↔ Romanized adaptation with facts unchanged |

## Examples

- "Write a launch landing page for Pahadi Maha raw honey: Rs 650 for 500 g, hives in Sindhupalchok, COD and eSewa, valley delivery Rs 100."
- "Edit this, keep every number: 'Nepal's No. 1 khata app!!! 100% guaranteed. Price inbox ma.'"
- "Yo tagline Nepali ma natural banaidinus: 'Keep your shop's udharo on your phone.'"

All brand names in examples (Sathi Khata, Pahadi Maha, Dhaka Ghar, Himali Brew, Kheti Saathi, SikshaPath) are fictional.

## How it works

Every skill works from your facts. Prices stay in NPR as you write them, dates stay in BS or AD as you give them, and missing details are listed rather than invented. Nepal context (digital landscape, payments and delivery, festival calendar, advertising and e-commerce rules) ships inside each skill's `references/` folder with sources and read dates, and skills tell you when a figure or rule may be out of date.

## What it will not do

- Invent prices, discounts, deadlines, stock levels, reviews, testimonials, follower counts or results.
- Write fake reviews, comments from fake accounts, undisclosed sponsored posts, or AI-generated "customers".
- Write content that mocks or stereotypes a caste, ethnicity, religion, region, language, gender or disability, or uses national symbols to sell.
- Give legal, tax or financial advice. Rules are flagged with their source so you can confirm them.
- Send messages, post, or spend money. You review and publish everything yourself.

## Data and network

The plugin contains only instructions and reference text; it ships no code and stores nothing. Skills fetch only URLs you give, and run a web search only when you ask for current research and your assistant has a search tool. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
