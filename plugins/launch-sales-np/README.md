# Nepal Launch Sales & Inquiries

Part of [Product Launching](../../README.md), Claude skills for launching products in Nepal, by ICT Matrics.

## What it does

Sales follow-through for a Nepal launch: saved replies and chat replies for Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, Viber and comments ('price?', 'COD milcha?', 'original ho?', size, haggling, confirmation, complaints) in Romanized Nepali, Nepali or English; sorting launch comments and inquiries into ready buyers, interested, resellers and complaints with reply drafts; consent-based SMS, Viber and email launch messages; and Daraz-style marketplace listings. Drafts only; never sends; no pressure tactics.

## Skills

Each skill is chosen by what you give it:

| You give | Skill |
|---|---|
| Your terms, or one chat to answer | `dm-sales-replies`: Saved-reply set or a ready reply, in the buyer's language |
| Pasted comments, chats or screenshots | `comments-leads`: Ready buyers, interested, resellers, complaints, with reply drafts for the best |
| Purpose, audience, offer | `launch-sms-viber`: Consent-based SMS, Viber, WhatsApp and email launch messages and sequences |
| Product facts and specs | `marketplace-listing`: Marketplace title, bullets, description, image shot list, Q&A answers |

## Examples

- "Make Messenger saved replies for our honey launch: Rs 650/500 g, COD, valley Rs 100 (1–2 days), outside Rs 200 (3–5 days)."
- "Sort these 40 comments on our launch post and draft replies for the ready buyers."
- "Launch ko SMS lekhidinus, existing customers lai, Roman script ma."

All brand names in examples (Sathi Khata, Pahadi Maha, Dhaka Ghar, Himali Brew, Kheti Saathi, SikshaPath) are fictional.

## How it works

Every skill works from your facts. Prices stay in NPR as you write them, dates stay in BS or AD as you give them, and missing details are listed rather than invented. Nepal context (digital landscape, payments and delivery, festival calendar, advertising and e-commerce rules) ships inside each skill's `references/` folder with sources and read dates, and skills tell you when a figure or rule may be out of date.

## What it will not do

- Invent prices, discounts, deadlines, stock levels, reviews, testimonials, follower counts or results.
- Write fake reviews, comments from fake accounts, undisclosed sponsored posts, or AI-generated "customers".
- Write content that mocks or stereotypes a caste, ethnicity, religion, region, language, gender or disability, or uses national symbols to sell.
- Give legal, tax or financial advice. Rules are flagged with their source so you can confirm them.
- Send messages, post, or spend money. You review and publish everything yourself.

## Data and network

The plugin contains only instructions and reference text; it ships no code and stores nothing. Skills fetch only URLs you give, and run a web search only when you ask for current research and your assistant has a search tool. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
