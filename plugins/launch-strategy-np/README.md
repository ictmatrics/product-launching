# Nepal Launch Strategy

Part of [Product Launching](../../README.md), Claude skills for launching products in Nepal, by ICT Matrics.

## What it does

Product launch strategy for the Nepal market: a launch plan with first buyers, positioning, channels, NPR budget and 90-day timeline; a readiness check covering the E-Commerce Act listing, payments, delivery, returns and trust signals; a Bikram Sambat launch calendar around Dashain, Tihar, Teej, New Year, wedding seasons and fiscal-year end; launch pricing and offers in NPR; and a post-launch retrospective from your own data. Nothing invented: no fake numbers, reviews or urgency.

## Skills

Each skill is chosen by what you give it:

| You give | Skill |
|---|---|
| Product facts, price, buyers, budget | `launch-plan`: Launch plan: first buyers, positioning, channel mix, NPR budget, pre-launch → launch week → 90 days, numbers to watch |
| A plan, a page or a short description | `launch-readiness-check`: Go / Go with fixes / Hold, with blocking items first |
| Category and target month | `launch-calendar-np`: Best window and a dated BS/AD calendar around festivals and courier cut-offs |
| Costs, competitor prices | `launch-pricing-np`: Unit economics in NPR, price position, launch offer options, honest price wording |
| Ads, sales, Messenger and courier data | `launch-retro`: Funnel, leaks (message, order, delivery, COD), next 30 days |

## Examples

- "Plan the launch of Sathi Khata, an Rs 299/month udharo app, for kirana shops in Rupandehi before Dashain."
- "Are we ready to launch? We sell dhaka kurtas on Facebook, COD only, no return policy yet."
- "Dashain ma honey launch garne, kahile suru garne ra kasto calendar banaune?"

All brand names in examples (Sathi Khata, Pahadi Maha, Dhaka Ghar, Himali Brew, Kheti Saathi, SikshaPath) are fictional.

## How it works

Every skill works from your facts. Prices stay in NPR as you write them, dates stay in BS or AD as you give them, and missing details are listed rather than invented. Nepal context (digital landscape, payments and delivery, festival calendar, advertising and e-commerce rules) ships inside each skill's `references/` folder with sources and read dates, and skills tell you when a figure or rule may be out of date.

## What it will not do

- Invent prices, discounts, deadlines, stock levels, reviews, testimonials, follower counts or results.
- Write fake reviews, comments from fake accounts, undisclosed sponsored posts, or AI-generated "customers".
- Write content that mocks or stereotypes a caste, ethnicity, religion, region, language, gender or disability, or uses national symbols to sell.
- Give legal, tax or financial advice. Rules are flagged with their source so you can confirm them.
- Send messages, post, or spend money. You review and publish everything yourself.

## Data and network

The plugin contains only instructions and reference text; it ships no code and stores nothing. Skills fetch only URLs you give, and run a web search only when you ask for current research and your assistant has a search tool. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
