# Nepal launch channel playbook

Pick channels by where the first buyers already are. Costs and results vary; never quote a cost per result unless the user supplies their own data. "Fits" lines are judgement, not data.

| Channel | Fits | Launch use | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook page + Meta ads | Almost every consumer product; most of Nepal's social audience is here | Announcement, offer posts, carousel of products, click-to-Messenger ads, retargeting video viewers | Comments asking "price?" must be answered fast; ads paid by dollar card; never rely on it alone |
| Messenger | Default help desk and order desk | Auto-reply with price, delivery and order steps; human follow-up within minutes in launch week | Slow replies lose orders; saved replies must match real terms |
| TikTok | Under-30 buyers, fashion, beauty, food, gadgets, cafés, education | Short demos, behind-the-scenes, creator duets, live selling | Trends change weekly; paid creator posts must be labelled |
| Instagram | Urban, premium, fashion, cafés, gifting, diaspora | Reels, product shots, story countdown to launch, DM orders | Smaller audience than Facebook in Nepal |
| YouTube | Explainers, reviews, tech, education, B2B demos | Long demo video, unboxing, tutorial in Nepali | Slower to build; good as a reference link in DMs |
| Viber / WhatsApp | Repeat buyers, B2B, dealers, family groups, diaspora | Launch broadcast to opted-in contacts, Viber community, order confirmations | Consent required; no spam blasts to bought lists |
| SMS (bulk SMS providers) | Older and rural buyers, existing customers, reminders | Launch day message, offer reminder, delivery updates | Short; include brand name and opt-out |
| Daraz and other marketplaces | Buyers searching to compare; nationwide logistics | Listing on launch day, launch voucher if the user sets one | Price comparison is instant; commission and campaign rules apply |
| Own website | Credibility, SEO, B2B, diaspora orders | Launch page, FAQ, policies, order form or checkout | Needs Google Business Profile and real contact details |
| Google Business Profile | Shops, cafés, clinics, services with an address | Show location, hours, photos, reviews from real customers | Address must be real; no fake reviews |
| Creators and influencers | Fashion, beauty, food, travel, tech, education | Unboxing, first-impression, "try it with me" videos | Mandatory sponsored disclosure (National Advertising Policy 2083); check their audience location |
| Dealers, distributors, retailers | FMCG, agriculture, hardware, electronics, outside-valley buyers | Dealer meeting, retailer margin and display, launch stock scheme | Credit terms, returns and margin must be decided before launch |
| Events and pop-ups | Food, fashion, crafts, cafés, tech | Bhatbhateni/mall activation, weekend markets, trade fairs, college fests | Permits and stall costs; collect consented contacts |
| FM radio and local newspapers | Outside-valley, older buyers, agriculture, local services | Launch jingle or announcement, sponsored segment | Advertisement Board rules on audio ads; check pre-approval |
| PR and Nepali tech/business media | Startups, apps, fintech, B2B | Launch story with real numbers | Never pay for "news" without labelling it as advertising |
| Cooperatives, associations, chambers | B2B, agriculture, finance, shop tools | Demo at a meeting, member offer | Slow decisions; relationships matter |

## Minimum viable launch mix for a small team

- Discovery: Facebook/Instagram ads plus one TikTok or creator push, or dealers for offline products.
- Conversation: Messenger plus a phone or Viber number that a person answers.
- Owned backup: a consented Viber community, SMS list or email list from day one.
- Trust: real address or pickup point, return terms, and early real reviews.
