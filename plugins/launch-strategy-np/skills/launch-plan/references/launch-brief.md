# Launch brief fields

| Field | Why it matters | Example (fictional: Sathi Khata) |
|---|---|---|
| Product in one sentence | Minimum to plan | "Android app for small shops to track udharo, stock and daily sales." |
| Price and payment | Positioning, offer, checkout | "Rs 299/month after 30-day free trial; eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay QR." |
| First buyers | Channel choice | "Kirana and hardware shop owners in Rupandehi and Kapilvastu." |
| What they use today | Positioning | "Paper khata notebook; some use Excel on a cousin's laptop." |
| Where it is sold | Channels and delivery | "Play Store download; field agents visit shops." |
| Launch date or season | Timeline | "Before Dashain 2084, roughly Bhadra." |
| Budget in NPR | Budget split | "Rs 1,50,000 for 3 months, including agent travel." |
| Team | Who runs what | "Founder, 1 marketer, 3 field agents, 1 support person." |
| Existing audience | Starting point | "Facebook page with 1,800 followers from the pilot." |
| Proof assets | Trust | "212 pilot shops; 6 owners agreed to be quoted with name and town." |
| Capacity limits | Promises | "Support 9–6 Sunday–Friday; agents cover Butwal and Bhairahawa only." |
| Registration status | Compliance | "Company registered, PAN yes, VAT no; e-commerce listing in progress." |
| Known competitors | Positioning | Only names the user gives |
| Constraints | Plan limits | "No TV; founder will not appear on camera." |

## Minimum to start

Product, price, first buyers, where it is sold, rough date. Everything else improves the plan; missing items become stated assumptions or open decisions.
