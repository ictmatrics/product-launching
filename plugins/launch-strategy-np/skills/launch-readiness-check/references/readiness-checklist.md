# Nepal launch readiness checklist

## A. Legal and registration (see compliance-np.md)
- A1. Business registered (company, firm or sole proprietorship) and PAN in place; VAT registration if required.
- A2. Listed on the Department of Commerce, Supplies and Consumer Protection e-commerce portal if selling online (E-Commerce Act, 2081), including social-media-only sellers.
- A3. Seller name, address, phone and listing number (if any) visible on the page, site or listing.
- A4. Sector approvals where relevant: DDA (medicines, supplements, Ayurvedic), DFTQC (food), education, finance, foreign employment, cooperatives, telecom devices.
- A5. Trademark: brand name not copying an existing mark; no use of another brand's logo or name without permission.
- A6. Audio-visual ads: checked whether Advertisement Board pre-approval applies.
- A7. Influencer agreements include sponsored disclosure.
- A8. Consent and privacy: buyers' data used only for orders unless they opt in to messages.

## B. Offer and price
- B1. Final price fixed; VAT inclusion stated if VAT-registered.
- B2. Launch offer has real terms and a real end date (in BS and AD if dates are used).
- B3. Any "was" price is a price the product was actually sold at.
- B4. Price comparable with Daraz and local alternatives, or the difference explained.

## C. Ordering and payment
- C1. Price visible in posts and listings (not only "inbox").
- C2. Payment methods ready and tested: QR (Fonepay/NepalPay), wallets (eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay), bank transfer, COD.
- C3. COD policy decided (where offered, advance if any, what happens on refusal).
- C4. Order flow tested end to end with a real test order.

## D. Delivery and after-sales
- D1. Delivery partner contracted; inside-valley and outside-valley charges and times known.
- D2. Festival and monsoon cut-off dates known.
- D3. Return, exchange and warranty terms written and visible.
- D4. Packaging survives courier handling; invoice or bill included.
- D5. Stock enough for the launch target plus a buffer; reorder lead time known.

## E. Trust signals
- E1. Real photos and videos of the product (not only supplier or stock images).
- E2. Physical address, shop or pickup point, or a clear explanation if online only.
- E3. Phone or Viber number answered by a person.
- E4. Real early reviews or testimonials with consent, or a plan to collect them.
- E5. Page name, profile picture, cover and "About" consistent across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google Business Profile and Daraz.

## F. Conversation readiness
- F1. Messenger auto-reply and saved replies with real price, delivery and order steps.
- F2. Someone assigned to reply during launch hours, including evenings.
- F3. Answers ready for the top objections: price, original or not, delivery time and charge, COD, size/fit, warranty, return.

## G. Channels and backup
- G1. Ad account set up; payment via bank e-com card or registered agency; card limit enough for the plan.
- G2. Tracking: Meta Pixel or conversions setup on the website if used; UTM links; a simple order sheet.
- G3. Owned backup channel ready (consented phone/Viber/SMS/email list, website or marketplace store).
- G4. Content bank for launch week ready.

## H. Claims
- H1. No unbacked "No. 1", "best", "cheapest", "100% original", health, organic or result claims (see claims-np.md).
- H2. No fake urgency, scarcity, reviews or AI "customers".
- H3. No content that mocks a caste, ethnicity, religion, region, language, gender or disability; national symbols used only if permitted.
