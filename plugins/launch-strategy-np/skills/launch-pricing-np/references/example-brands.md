# Fictional example brands

All examples in this repository use these invented businesses. Any resemblance to a real business is accidental. Never present them to a user as real, and never borrow their facts for the user's copy.

| Brand | What it sells | Where | Typical launch problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sathi Khata** | Mobile app for kirana and hardware shops to track udharo (credit), stock and daily sales; Rs 299/month after a 30-day free trial | Butwal, selling nationwide | Shopkeepers trust their notebook; need proof it works offline and in Nepali |
| **Pahadi Maha** | Raw wild honey, 250 g and 500 g jars | Hives in Sindhupalchok, bottled in Kathmandu | Buyers suspect adulterated honey; "pure" claims everywhere |
| **Dhaka Ghar** | Modern kurtas, shawls and jackets in hand-woven dhaka fabric | Lalitpur workshop, ships nationwide and abroad | Seasonal (Dashain, Teej, weddings); size and exchange worries |
| **Himali Brew** | Single-origin coffee beans and drip bags from Gulmi and Arghakhanchi farms | Pokhara roastery | Coffee seen as imported luxury; needs to win cafés and gift buyers |
| **Kheti Saathi** | Drip-irrigation kits for small vegetable farmers | Chitwan; dealers in the Terai | Buyers are offline and price-sensitive; dealers sell, not ads |
| **SikshaPath** | Online Loksewa (public service exam) preparation classes | Kathmandu, students nationwide | Crowded education market; result claims are restricted |

## Sample facts for Sathi Khata (used in several skill examples)

- Works on Android; works offline and syncs when the phone gets internet.
- Interface in Nepali and English.
- Sends SMS reminders to customers who owe money, if the shopkeeper chooses.
- 30-day free trial, no payment details needed to start; then Rs 299/month, payable by eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay QR.
- 212 shops in Rupandehi used it during the pilot (Shrawan–Asoj 2083).
- No iPhone app yet.
- Support by phone and Viber, 9 am to 6 pm, Sunday to Friday.
