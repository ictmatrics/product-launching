# Privacy policy: Nepal Launch Social & Ads

Effective date: 2026-11-20.

Nepal Launch Social & Ads is a set of instructions and reference documents for an AI assistant. It contains no code that runs, no server, no analytics and no tracking. ICT Matrics receives nothing from your use of it.

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| Categories of personal data collected | None. |
| Purposes of processing | None, because nothing is collected. |
| Recipients or third parties | None. The plugin sends data nowhere. |
| Retention | None. The plugin stores nothing. |
| Your controls | None needed. Remove the plugin at any time; nothing remains. |

## What happens when you use it

- Some skills fetch public pages at URLs you give, and only those. A web search runs only when you ask for current research and your assistant has a search tool. Fetching and searching are done by the assistant's own tools under the assistant provider's privacy terms.
- Text, chats, comments and files you paste stay in your conversation with the assistant, under the assistant provider's privacy terms.
- In answers, skills refer to buyers and private people by role or label and do not copy their phone numbers, addresses or account ids. Remove personal data you do not want in the conversation before pasting customer chats or order sheets.
- Messaging skills write drafts for consented contacts only and never send anything.
- Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 applies to how you, the business, handle customers' data.
