# Launch brief: Sathi Khata (fictional)

- **Product:** Android app for kirana and hardware shops to track udharo (credit), stock and daily sales.
- **Works offline:** yes, syncs when the phone gets internet.
- **Languages:** Nepali and English interface.
- **Reminders:** sends SMS reminders to customers who owe money, only to customers the shopkeeper selects.
- **Price:** 30-day free trial, no payment details needed; then Rs 299/month. Payment by eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay QR. Not VAT-registered.
- **First buyers:** kirana and hardware shop owners in Rupandehi and Kapilvastu.
- **What they use today:** paper khata notebook; a few use Excel on a relative's laptop.
- **Proof:** pilot Shrawan–Asoj 2083 with 212 shops in Rupandehi. Six owners agreed to be quoted with name and town. One quote (Customer A, kirana shop, Butwal): "Pahila Dashain agadi udharo uthauna 2 hapta lagthyo, yo patak 4 din ma sakiyo."
- **Not available:** no iPhone app yet; no multi-shop accounts yet.
- **Team:** founder, 1 marketer, 3 field agents (Butwal and Bhairahawa only), 1 support person.
- **Support:** phone and Viber, 9 am–6 pm, Sunday–Friday.
- **Existing audience:** Facebook page with 1,800 followers.
- **Budget:** Rs 1,50,000 for 3 months, including agent travel.
- **Launch timing:** wants to launch before Dashain 2084.
- **Registration:** private limited company registered, PAN yes; e-commerce portal listing in progress.
- **Brief also says (check this):** "Nepal's No. 1 khata app, trusted by thousands of shops."
