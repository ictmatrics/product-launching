# Notice and credits

Product Launching is derived in part from **marketing-skills** by Sergey Skripko, https://github.com/grayskripko/marketing-skills, released under the MIT License. The original copyright notice is kept in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## What came from the original

- The skills-only plugin format and marketplace layout (`.claude-plugin/`, `.codex-plugin/`, `skills/*/SKILL.md`, `references/`).
- The evidence-first approach: ground rules against fabrication, fact locks, claims ledgers, and stock-phrasing checks.
- The Copywriting Editor Kit skills (`landing-page-copy`, `copy-edit`, `copy-diagnose`, `copy-proof-check`, `brand-voice`), adapted here in `plugins/launch-copy-np` with Nepal-specific rules, examples, claims and stock-phrasing catalogues.
- The comment-sorting idea from the LinkedIn Warm Leads plugin, rebuilt here as `comments-leads` for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Messenger.

## What is new in Product Launching

- A launch-centred structure for the Nepal market across five plugins: strategy, copy, social and ads, sales, and SEO.
- Nepal reference material with sources and read dates: digital landscape, payments and delivery, the Bikram Sambat festival and buying calendar, the E-Commerce Act 2081, Advertisement (Regulation) Act 2076, National Advertising Policy 2083 and related rules.
- New skills: launch-plan, launch-readiness-check, launch-calendar-np, launch-pricing-np, launch-retro, bilingual-copy, social-launch-posts, meta-ad-copy, short-video-script, influencer-brief, dm-sales-replies, launch-sms-viber, marketplace-listing, launch-page-seo, nepali-keyword-map, local-seo-check, ai-search-visibility.
- Fictional Nepali example brands throughout.

## Not carried over

The original's SEO audit, AI SEO and Google search-update plugins are not included as-is; the parts relevant to a launch were rewritten for Nepal in `plugins/launch-seo-np`. Use the original repository for the full global SEO toolset.
