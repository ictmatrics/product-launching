# Claims that need evidence (Nepal launch edition)

The phrases in quotes below are examples of claims to check, never copy to use. Each claim either gets evidence from the user, a weaker honest version, or is removed.

## Claim types common in Nepali launch copy

| Type | Examples seen in Nepali ads and posts | What backs it |
|---|---|---|
| Rankings and superlatives | "No. 1 in Nepal", "Nepal's best", "sabai bhanda sasto", "सबैभन्दा राम्रो", "leading brand" | A named, dated ranking or survey; otherwise remove |
| Price claims | "Cheapest in Nepal", "factory price", "50% off" | A real comparison the user ran; for discounts, the real previous price the product was actually sold at, and the period |
| Originality and origin | "100% original", "genuine product", "imported from Korea", "Japanese technology", "made in Nepal" | Authorized-distributor letter, import documents, or the user's own production facts |
| Health and body claims | "cures gastric", "weight loss guaranteed", "doctor recommended", "Ayurvedic treatment", "chemical-free" | DDA/DFTQC registration and a qualified source; cure or treatment claims need regulatory approval. Flag, never write without it |
| Organic and natural | "organic", "100% natural", "pure Himalayan" | A certificate (name, issuer, year) or the user's own sourcing facts stated plainly ("honey from our hives in Sindhupalchok") |
| Certifications and marks | "NS certified", "ISO certified", "DDA approved", "award-winning" | Certificate name, number or issuer, and year |
| Customer counts and social proof | "10,000+ happy customers", "Nepal's most trusted", "1 lakh followers" | A current count and what counts as a customer; followers are not customers |
| Testimonials and reviews | quotes, screenshots of chats, star ratings, "customer feedback" | Real customers who agreed to be quoted; chat screenshots need the customer's consent and their number hidden |
| Influencer and celebrity endorsements | "as used by [creator]", creator videos | A real agreement; paid posts must be self-declared as sponsored (National Advertising Policy 2083, clause 9.13) |
| Urgency and scarcity | "limited stock", "only 5 left", "offer ends tonight", "Dashain offer last day" | A real stock count or real end date in the user's plan |
| Delivery promises | "free delivery all over Nepal", "same-day delivery", "24-hour delivery" | The user's real courier terms, with conditions (inside valley only, minimum order) |
| Guarantees and warranty | "1 year warranty", "money-back guarantee", "lifetime guarantee" | The actual terms, who honours them, and how to claim |
| Results | "double your sales", "get a job abroad", "100% visa success", "guaranteed marks" | A named, scoped case; in education, employment and finance these claims are restricted, so flag |

## Writing a claim honestly

- **Scope it.** "In our first 3 months, 212 shops in Butwal started using Sathi Khata" beats "thousands of shops trust us".
- **Match strength to evidence.** One shop's result supports "helped one shop", not "helps every business".
- **Use the real count** the user gives, written the Nepali way if the user prefers (1,20,000).
- **Replace hype with checkable detail.** "Honey from our own hives in Sindhupalchok, bottled in Kathmandu" is more believable to Nepali buyers than "100% pure".
- **Name the missing proof** instead of filling the gap: "needs the import document before saying 'original'".

## When the user wants testimonials but has none

Never write fake testimonials, fake chat screenshots, fake comments or fake star ratings, and never suggest posting from fake accounts. Offer a message the user can send to real early buyers, in the buyer's language:

> Namaste! Hamro [product] prayog garnu bhayeko ma dhanyabad. Ek-dui line ma bhannu huncha — pahila ke prayog garnu hunthyo, ra [product] le ke farak paryo? Tapainko naam ra thau sahit hamro page ma rakhna milcha? Naam narakhna bhanna pani paunu huncha.

Nepali (Devanagari) version:

> नमस्ते! हाम्रो [product] प्रयोग गर्नुभएकोमा धन्यवाद। एक–दुई लाइनमा भन्नुहोस् न — पहिले के प्रयोग गर्नुहुन्थ्यो, र [product] ले के फरक पार्‍यो? तपाईंको नाम र ठाउँसहित हाम्रो पेजमा राख्न मिल्छ? नाम नराख्न भन्न पनि पाउनुहुन्छ।

## Why evidence matters in Nepal

The Advertisement (Regulation) Act, 2076 prohibits misleading advertisements, the Consumer Protection Act, 2075 protects buyers from misleading information, and the National Advertising Policy, 2083 adds disclosure duties for sponsored and influencer content and regulation of AI-generated ads. Details and read dates: `compliance-np.md`. This plugin checks claims against the user's evidence; it does not give legal advice or clearance.
